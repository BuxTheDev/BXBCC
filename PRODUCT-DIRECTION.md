# BXB product direction

Vision → identity → priorities → execution → results → reflection → adjustment.

The product answers “What deserves Bryan’s attention right now?” Cozii and Valley of Grace are the two primary entrepreneurial fronts. Other records remain available without equal dashboard prominence. Ideas enter an Opportunity Inbox; preparation creates an incubating front, never an automatic active commitment.

The North Star is a user-defined $30M net worth target by age 30. The date is intentionally unset until supplied. Recorded financial position is not a forecast or a complete verified valuation. KPI readings, constraints, and dated objectives must be entered by the user.

This first phase adds a calm Home/Today, a goal card, a daily intention and three shared focus tasks, HQ business scorecards, an Opportunity Inbox, Knowing → Doing with evidence history, and decision/reflection prompts. Existing asset, entity, financial, CRM, task, and recovery tools remain available.

On first load, if both named businesses exist and are neither completed nor scrapped, focus migration ranks Cozii first and Valley of Grace second and moves other active fronts into incubation. IDs and linked records are retained. A full pre-update local backup is written before the change, available in Settings. The migration runs once per workspace. Cloud sync retains its existing whole-workspace conflict checks.

Future page-by-page work: dedicated Cozii operating metrics, Valley of Grace house launch playbooks, allocation records, and structured CEO review fields. This phase does not implement resident management, reservations, legal compliance rules, valuation forecasts, or bank integrations.

Validation: `node --test tests/direction.test.cjs`, production build, existing backup/sync checks, browser persistence and promotion/activation checks, and mobile layout review.
